The SIX experience

Six microphones.
An entire band.

SIX doesn’t simply sing songs. The brothers build drums, bass, rhythm, melody and harmony live—then turn the space between songs into its own kind of family entertainment.

No tracks pretending to be instruments. No band hidden backstage. Just six voices, years of instinct and the unmistakable chemistry of brothers who know exactly how to make one another laugh.

Good questions

Everything guests ask before they say yes.

Do they really make every sound with their voices?+

Yes. The core promise is simple: six voices create the sound of an entire band live, without instrumental accompaniment.

Is SIX a concert or a comedy show?+

It is a two-act vocal-band experience built from music, live sound, family chemistry, heart and naturally occurring brother comedy.

Is the show a good fit for different generations?+

The musical range and family connection are designed to give multiple generations something to recognize, enjoy and talk about afterward.

Where does SIX perform?+

SIX performs at Pepsi Legends Theater, 1600 W 76 Country Blvd in Branson, Missouri. Live dates will be connected to the approved ticket calendar.
